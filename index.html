Background

About Me

I'm a web administrator with hands-on experience managing both public-facing and internal web systems. My work sits at the intersection of content management, front-end development, and long-term site maintenance — the kind of work that doesn't make headlines but keeps things running well.

I believe a well-structured HTML file is worth more than a complicated framework that no one can maintain in two years. I lean toward clear naming conventions, readable code, and designs that hold up under real-world use.

I manage CMS-driven websites alongside legacy intranet systems, handling everything from content updates and template edits to accessibility improvements and responsive upgrades. My approach is practical first, elegant where possible.

When I take on a project, I'm thinking about the person who has to maintain it after I'm done — whether that's me in three years or someone else entirely.
